# Luz en Once - Development Summary

This document details the features, fixes, and improvements implemented during the recent development session.

## 1. Audio System & Combat Feel
We implemented a centralized `AudioManager` and integrated it into the player's actions to enhance game feel.

### **Features Added:**
- **Dynamic Footsteps:** The player now triggers footstep sounds based on movement speed (Walk vs. Run intervals).
- **Combat Impact:** 
  - **Audio:** Punch and Kick actions trigger specific sound effects.
  - **Screen Shake:** Hitting an enemy now causes the camera to shake, adding weight to and feedback to combat.
  - **Visuals:** Damage numbers (if implemented in UI) and state transitions are synchronized.

**Key Components:**
- `AudioManager.gd` (Singleton)
- `PlayerController.gd` (Footstep and Hit logic)
- `OrbitCamera.gd` (Screen Shake)

## 2. Enemy AI Improvements
The generic `NPCController` was upgraded with a robust Line-of-Sight and Hearing system.

### **Enhancements:**
- **Vision Cone:** Enemies now have a `vision_angle` and `detection_range`. They logic allows them to ignore the player if they are behind them or out of range.
- **Hearing:** Running or attacking near an enemy can trigger detection even without visual contact.
- **Memory:** Enemies retain "Last Known Position" logic when losing sight of the player, investigating the last spot they saw them.

## 3. Map Visuals (City Generator)
We enhanced the `city_generator.gd` to produce a more vibrant, "Once-style" aesthetic, matching the game's theme.

### **Visual Upgrades:**
- **Varied Palettes:** Added specific color arrays for "Residencial" (creams, beiges) and "Comercial" (vibrant yellows, oranges) buildings.
- **Street Details:** Added road markings (yellow center lines, white stop lines) and distinct sidewalk textures.
- **Urban Furniture:** Procedural placement of street lamps (`_crear_poste`) and trees in plazas.

## 4. Animation & Physics Fixes
We reverted problematic "Root Motion Extraction" settings that were causing the character to sink into the ground or slide, restoring stable movement.
- **Reverted:** `sync_locomotion_anim_speed` logic was removed to restore natural animation playback.
- **Reverted:** `root_motion_track` in `luz.tscn` was cleared to prevent the mesh from detaching from the physics capsule.

## 5. Enemy Visuals & Configuration
### Visuals
- **Model Update:** Swapped `boliviandude.fbx` for `frontierfriend.fbx`.
- **Scaling:** Increased enemy scale to **4x** (set in `NPCController._ready`) to match game world proportions.
- **Textures:** Removed the red debug material override to allow the new model's native textures to display.

### AI Vision Fixes
- **Raycast Logic:** Fixed `VisionRay` target calculation. It now correctly uses `to_local()` to point at the player's position relative to the enemy's rotation.
- **Collision Mask:** Updated `VisionRay` to detect both the World (walls) and the Player's Hurtbox (Layer 4), ensuring enemies can see the player but are blocked by obstacles.
- **Targeting:** Adjusted ray target to aim at the player's chest (1m height) for better line-of-sight reliability.

## 6. Combat Scoring System
We implemented a dynamic scoring system that rewards the player for aggressive combat.

### **Features:**
- **Hit Scoring:** Points are awarded every time an enemy is hit and knocked back. Stronger knockbacks (force > 10) award more points.
- **Kill Scoring:** Bonus points are awarded when an enemy is defeated.
- **Combo System:** Existing combo multiplier in `ScoreManager` now applies to hit points as well.

**Scoring Reference:**
- **Weak Hit:** 10 points (+ combo multiplier)
- **Strong Hit:** 50 points (+ combo multiplier)
- **Kill:** 100-500 points depending on enemy type.
